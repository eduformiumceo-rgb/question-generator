# WASSCE style: what each subject is based on

Every figure below is generated from `WASSCE_PROFILES` in `src/wassceStyle.js`, and `tests/wassceStyle.test.js` checks that each paper's marks add up to the total printed here.

**How structures were established**
1. The 32 supplied WASSCE (School Candidates) papers: the 2025 papers and the 2021 papers (read by OCR and by looking at the pages).
2. WAEC's published syllabus schemes of examination, used to confirm those papers and to fill subjects with no supplied paper.
Each profile lists its `sources` and anything still assumed under `unverified`.

## Subjects with no official structure on file
Arabic, Spanish, Performing Arts, Picture Making, Art and Design Studio, Applied Electricity, Applied Technology, Auto Mechanics, Building Construction, Metalwork, Woodwork, Technical Drawing, Electronics, Engineering, Robotics and the other technical and new-curriculum electives use a generic WASSCE shape (Paper 1: 50 objective questions; Paper 2: Section A one compulsory 25-mark question, Section B any 3 of 5 x 25). The generator screen tells the teacher this and asks them to check the sections. Religious and Moral Education and Physical Education are not WASSCE subjects.

## What the layout does
- Paper order per subject; Paper 1 in two columns where the real paper has them (Biology, Elective Maths).
- Paper 1: real instruction wording, worked example with the shaded bubble, bold command words.
- Paper 2 (separate booklets): its own cover page with code box, candidate boxes, instructions and the examiner's table.
- Essay pages: italic part labels, `[n marks]`, answer lines (where the booklet prints them), the "Do not write in this margin" column.
- Markup in question text: `**bold**`, `{{italic}}`, `_underline_`, pipe tables, `## heading` lines (printed part headings such as Elective Maths Parts I to III).
- The Word export has the same covers, tables and formatting, Paper 1 in two columns where applicable, and the margin rule and note on essay pages.
- Generated sections are repaired (numbering, option letters, unmatched markup) and checked (four options, a valid answer letter, sub-part marks) before they are accepted; a failed check triggers a retry.

## Known limits
- Practical papers (Paper 3) are not generated.
- English Literature (Part B of Paper 1) has one set of ten questions per genre; the real paper gives two alternative sets.
- Where WAEC weights a Paper 1 differently from its question count (for example History and Business Management), the paper prints the question count, as the supplied papers do.
- English Paper 1 has eight objective sections, so one generation makes more AI calls than other subjects.
