# WASSCE style: what was verified, subject by subject

Source: the 32 WASSCE (School Candidates) papers supplied. All were scans; they were read by OCR and by looking at the pages.

Subjects with no supplied paper (e.g. Food and Nutrition, Management in Living, Clothing and Textiles, French, History, the technical and visual-arts subjects) use a generic WASSCE shape: Paper 1 of 50 MCQ and a 100-mark essay paper. They are marked `generic: true` and should be checked against a real paper before being relied on.

## Known limits
- Paper 3 (practical) papers are not generated.
- English Literature (Part B of Paper 1) has one set of ten questions per genre; the real paper gives two alternative sets.
- Integrated Science Paper 1 duration (1 hour) is assumed.
- The "Do not write in this margin" column and two-column layout are rendered in the on-screen/printed paper; the Word export has the tables, markup, covers and answer lines but is single-column for Paper 1 and has no margin column.
- English Paper 1 has eight objective sections, so one generation makes more AI calls than other subjects.
